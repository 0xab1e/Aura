# Staff Software Engineer — Embedded Software
**Company:** Texas Instruments (TI)
**Location:** (International / Flexible)

---

## About the Role

Design and develop innovative embedded software solutions driving TI's revolutionary product lines. Write code implementing new and novel use cases for wireless microcontrollers. Contribute to OS and driver support. Collaborate with business partners, customers, marketing, systems and applications engineering teams.

---

## Minimum Requirements

- 7–10+ years of relevant experience
- Master's Degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field
- Expertise with **Embedded C programming**
- Expertise with **assembly language programming**
- Expertise with **real-time operating systems** such as FreeRTOS or Zephyr
- Expertise with **ARM Cortex-M series microcontrollers**
- Expertise with instrumentation equipment such as **logic analyzers**
- Expertise with debugging methodology:
  - Breakpoints, watchpoints
  - CPU exception decoding
  - Single instruction stepping
- Expertise in scripting languages such as **Python or JavaScript**
- Knowledge of **continuous integration systems** (Jenkins or similar)
- Knowledge of **build systems** (CMake or Make)
- Knowledge of **Linux/Unix systems**
